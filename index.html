Privacy Policy — Coloring Game - Art Puzzles

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This game is developed by comcot studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and what choices you have.

We do not ask you for personal information

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no place to type your name, e-mail, or any other detail about you. We never collect your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, or precise location.

What is collected automatically

The game uses three services provided by Google:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase records in-game events — for example that a level started, was completed, or was abandoned — together with your device model, operating system version, an app instance identifier, and the country derived from your IP address. We use this to see which levels are too hard and where players stop playing.
  • Firebase Crashlytics records technical details when the game crashes: the error, the state of the device, and an installation identifier. We use this to fix crashes.
  • Google AdMob shows advertisements. It may use your device's advertising identifier, your IP address, and information about the ads you see and tap.

Your progress stays on your device

Completed levels, boosters, and sound settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised advertising, and you may refuse. Anywhere in the world you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads), which stops advertisers from linking future activity to past activity.

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google on our behalf under Google's retention settings. To delete the data stored on your device, uninstall the game. To ask about data held by us, write to the address below.

How Google uses this data

Described in Google's own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.
